# SEO Audit

Use one canonical route registry as the comparison source.

1. Enumerate intended public routes, redirects, and explicit nonindexable surfaces.
2. Verify status codes, self-referencing canonicals, titles, descriptions, robots directives, sitemap membership, and redirect targets.
3. Validate structured data against visible content and require provenance for business, service-area, credential, testimonial, and review claims.
4. Check rendered HTML, internal links, heading structure, image alternatives, mobile behavior, and performance-sensitive client boundaries.
5. Confirm 404 and authenticated routes are noncanonical and nonindexable.
6. Add deterministic tests for every repaired route or registry invariant.

Do not edit Google Business Profile, publish claims, or change production domains without the named approval gate.
