## Platform

web

## Users

The primary user is a Twin Cities homeowner evaluating an interior or exterior repaint, often while concerned about mess, disruption, unclear scope, unreliable scheduling, or a finish that may fail prematurely.

Secondary users are property managers, small commercial and facility operators, and qualified public-sector buyers who need written scope, insurance clarity, safety awareness, and responsive coordination.

## Product Purpose

The public website helps prospective customers understand Sky's the Limit Painting LLC, evaluate its process and fit, and request an owner-led walkthrough or written estimate. Success means generating qualified project conversations while giving visitors enough process detail to make a confident decision.

## Positioning

Sky's the Limit is an owner-led, prep-first painting contractor. Anthony Briseno remains involved from walkthrough and written scope through preparation, execution, and final detail review. The differentiator is visible accountability and a documented preparation process, not an unsupported claim of superior quality.

## Operating Context

Projects begin with surfaces, existing condition, access, protection needs, preparation requirements, dimensions or photos, location, and timing. Work may include cleaning, masking, protecting, scraping, sanding, patching, caulking, priming, coating, cleanup, and a final walkthrough. Residential work is the primary homepage context; commercial, property, striping, and public-sector capabilities require their own qualified paths.

## Product Principles

1. Show the work that happens before the finish.
2. Make owner accountability visible from first contact to final walkthrough.
3. Prefer written scope and verified proof over broad quality claims.
4. Keep the homeowner's next action obvious while preserving direct call and text paths.
5. Separate residential conversion from commercial and public-sector qualification when their evidence requirements differ.
