## Scope and mode

Persuade mode for the public homepage and its immediate estimate path. Residential homeowners arriving from local search are primary; commercial and public-sector visitors keep qualified navigation paths without competing with the homepage action.

## Audience, job, action, and proof

The visitor needs to decide whether Sky's the Limit will protect the property, prepare surfaces correctly, communicate directly, and provide a clear scope. The primary action is **Book a free walkthrough**. Direct call and text remain secondary actions. Proof is the visible process, Anthony's direct involvement, neutral registration language, written-scope behavior, and only assets cleared for the use in which they appear.

## Chosen direction

**The Owner's Finish Ledger — Split Ledger composition.** The page is a continuous owner-managed project record: warm plaster paper, a visible construction grid, ink typography, cobalt masking tape, safety-orange action, and full-scale jobsite photography. It refuses the category's dark photo-overlay hero and repeated service-card grid.

Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/owners-finish-ledger-a.png`.

The memorable moment is a cobalt tape seam joining the written scope to the jobsite image, then carrying the visitor into the five-stage preparation ledger. Motion reveals stages along that seam once; reduced motion renders the complete record statically.

## Composition contract

- Desktop first viewport: light utility strip and navigation; 58/42 paper/photo hero; headline owns most of the paper field; primary action stays below the supporting line; the five-stage ledger begins before the fold ends.
- Mobile first viewport: offer, locality, and walkthrough action appear before the photo; the tape seam becomes a horizontal divider; stages remain full-size and scroll-snap rather than shrinking into microtype.
- The generated handwritten notes, exact line breaks, and generated factual wording are not literal claims. All text remains semantic and uses verified repository/product copy.
- Commercial and public-sector capability appears after residential process proof or in navigation, never in the primary headline.

## Sampled visual system

| Role | Comp sample | Use |
|---|---:|---|
| Plaster paper | `#F6F3EB` | Dominant page and navigation field |
| Ledger ink | `#071321` | Headings, body, structural rules |
| Masking blue | `#0254C3` | Tape seam, annotations, focus, process indices |
| Safety orange | `#FF661C` | Primary action and one process signal |
| Warm rule | `rgba(7, 19, 33, 0.18)` | Grid and section divisions |

Corners are 0px. Rules are 1px by default and 2px only for the tape-adjacent active line. Elevation is absent; hierarchy comes from scale, overlap, fields, and rules. Display type is a highly compressed grotesk; body type is a calm humanist sans. Desktop display ranges from 72–112px, mobile 48–68px; body copy remains 16–20px; labels remain at least 12px with restrained tracking.
