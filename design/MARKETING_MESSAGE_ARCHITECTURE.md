# Marketing Message Architecture

## Homepage

### Headline direction

**Painting built on preparation—not promises.**

### Supporting message

Owner-operated painting for Twin Cities homes, commercial properties, and qualified facility opportunities, with clear scope, visible preparation, and a direct next step.

### Primary action

**Check your project range**

### Proof immediately available

- Owner-operated
- Fully insured
- MN Specialty Contractor — Painting
- Contractor ID IR816596
- Real project work only

## Residential

**A cleaner finish starts before the first coat.**

Show protection, patching, sanding, masking, caulking, priming, and communication. Do not reduce the value proposition to color selection.

## Commercial

**Repaints planned around the way your property operates.**

Lead with access, occupancy, scheduling, protection, documentation, and closeout.

## Public sector / facilities

**Documented scope. Controlled preparation. Clear closeout.**

Lead with verified qualification facts and operational discipline. Do not imply contract awards or certifications that are not verified.

## Estimate

**Tell us enough to make the next conversation useful.**

Explain why each question matters. Clearly label the planning range as preliminary and nonbinding.

## Projects

**Real surfaces. Real preparation. Real result.**

Every project page must identify the challenge, preparation, execution, and result, with provenance.

## Portal

**Your project, documents, and next action in one place.**

Avoid dashboard jargon. Customer screens should always explain what changed and what action is available.

## Operator

**Work the next important thing.**

The cockpit is not a reporting museum. Every metric links to a record or action.

## CTA vocabulary

Use:

- Check project range
- Start scope review
- Request capability conversation
- View verified work
- Approve proposal
- Sign agreement
- Pay securely
- Review change
- Open next action

Avoid:

- Learn more
- Get started
- Elevate your space
- Unlock potential
- Experience excellence
